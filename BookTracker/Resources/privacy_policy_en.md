# BookTracker Privacy Policy

BookTracker values user privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed in the BookTracker app and how it is protected.

## Article 1. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

BookTracker processes personal information for the following purposes.

1. Providing account registration and login features
2. Identifying and managing user accounts
3. Storing and synchronizing user data such as books, collections, receipts, and reading records
4. Providing reading statistics, calendars, and reports
5. Responding to inquiries and improving the service
6. Processing account deletion and data deletion requests

## Article 2. Personal Information We Process

BookTracker may process the following information to provide the service.

1. Account information: email address and login provider information
2. Profile information: name, profile image, or avatar information
3. Service usage information: registered books, collections, receipts, reading records, ratings, reviews, memos, and statistics data
4. Local data: recent search keywords, temporary receipt data, and other data stored on the device
5. Inquiry information: email address and inquiry details provided by the user

BookTracker does not collect government-issued identification numbers such as resident registration numbers, passport numbers, or driver's license numbers.

## Article 3. Retention and Use Period

Personal information is retained while the user uses the BookTracker service.

When a user deletes their account, server data linked to the account will be deleted and cannot be restored.

However, if certain information must be retained under applicable laws, it may be stored for the period required by those laws.

## Article 4. Deletion of Personal Information

BookTracker deletes personal information without delay when the purpose of retention has been fulfilled or when the user requests account deletion.

Data stored on the server will be deleted, and local data stored on the device can be deleted through the in-app data deletion feature or by deleting the app.

Some data may remain for a certain period depending on backup, log, or external service retention policies.

## Article 5. Local Data and Server Data

BookTracker may store some data locally on the user's device to provide the service.

Recent search keywords, temporary receipt data, and similar information may be stored as local data and can be deleted through in-app features.

Books, collections, receipts, reading records, profile information, and other data linked to an account may be stored on the server.

## Article 6. Third-Party Sharing

BookTracker does not sell or arbitrarily provide users' personal information to third parties.

However, information may be provided within the necessary scope if required by law or with the user's consent.

## Article 7. Use of External Services

BookTracker may use the following external services to provide the service.

1. Apple: Apple login
2. Google: Google login
3. Supabase: user authentication, data storage, and server functionality
4. Book search APIs: book search and book information

External services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## Article 8. Possible International Transfer of Personal Information

BookTracker may use global external services such as Supabase, Apple, and Google. In this process, some data may be processed or stored on servers outside the user's country.

If international transfer occurs, the data will be handled according to the policies and security standards of the relevant external services.

## Article 9. Security Measures

BookTracker applies the following measures to protect users' personal information.

1. Login-based user authentication
2. User-specific data access control
3. Server security features and access permission management
4. Processing personal information only within the necessary scope
5. Account deletion and data deletion features

## Article 10. User Rights

Users have the following rights regarding their personal information.

1. Access personal information
2. Correct personal information
3. Delete personal information
4. Delete account
5. Request data reset

Users can exercise these rights through features provided in the app or by contacting the email address below.

## Article 11. Children's Privacy

BookTracker is not intended for children under the age of 14.

If we confirm that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected, we may delete such information without delay.

## Article 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The developer may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

Changes may be announced through the app screen or other notice methods.
