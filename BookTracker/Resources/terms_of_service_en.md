## Article 1. Purpose of the Service

BookTracker is an app designed to help users record and manage their reading activities.

Users can also record purchase or rental receipts and review their reading activity through collections and reading statistics.

## Article 5. Receipts and Collection Features

The receipt feature in BookTracker is used to record books that users have purchased or rented.

When a purchase receipt is created, the related books may be automatically added to the Purchased Books collection. When a rental receipt is created, the related books may be automatically added to the Borrowed Books collection.

Automatically created purchase/rental collections are system-managed collections, so their names and descriptions cannot be edited directly.

Statistics are calculated based on the data entered by users, so results may differ from actual records if the entered information is inaccurate.

Calendar and report images can be saved or shared directly by the user.
